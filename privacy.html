Talya Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Talya helps people hear how their tone of voice comes across. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Talya website or the Talya app for Android or iPhone.

The short version

  • Your voice recordings are analyzed on your own device and are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • We do not have accounts, so we do not collect your name, email address or phone number.
  • We do not use ads, trackers or analytics, and we never sell data.

Microphone and recordings

Talya asks for microphone access only so it can analyze a clip you choose to record. The audio is processed in the app on your device to measure pace, pitch, loudness and pauses. The audio is kept in memory only while the app is open and is discarded when you leave or reload. It is never saved to a server.

What is stored on your device

To show your progress, Talya saves the following in your browser or app storage, on your device only: the measurements from your calm baseline, the results of your checks and drills (for example pace and tone tag, not audio), your practice streak, and your sharing settings. You can delete all of it at any time from Our space → Delete everything on this device, or by uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data.

Sharing

Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share it. If you use the share card or the partner invite, you decide where it goes, and the app or service you send it to handles it under its own privacy policy. The share card contains a summary of your week and no audio.

Children

Talya is meant for adults 18 and over and is not directed to children.

Not medical advice

Talya describes how a way of speaking often comes across. It is not therapy, does not diagnose anything, and can be wrong.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If we ever add accounts or any data leaving your device, we will ask first.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/bawcejawn/Talya/issues.
