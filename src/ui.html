Talya

hear how you sound, together

Talya listens to how something is said (pace, pitch, loudness), not what is meant. Results describe how a delivery often comes across. They can be wrong, and they are not a diagnosis or a verdict on who is right.

New check

Speak for 5 to 60 seconds. Your audio is analyzed on this device and never uploaded.

Clips this session

    Prep a hard conversation

    Plan your first two sentences. The first minute often sets the tone for the rest.

    Your opening

    Before you start

    • Ask first: "Is now an okay time to talk about something?"
    • Pick a time when neither of you is hungry, rushing, or just home.
    • Agree that either of you can call a 20-minute break.

    Rehearse it out loud

    Goal: warm or soft delivery, at or below your usual pace.

    Practice

    Short drills with a measurable goal. Record the line out loud and Talya checks your delivery against the goal. Goals compare to your baseline when you have one.
