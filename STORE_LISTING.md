## Full description
Ever said something reasonable and still started a fight? Often it isn't the words. It's the delivery.

Talya plays back how your tone of voice comes across. Record yourself saying something, and Talya measures your pace, pitch, loudness and pauses, then tells you in plain language how it may land with your partner.

HOW IT WORKS
• Record your calm voice once. Talya compares everything to it.
• Check a real moment: say it the way you said it, or the way you're about to.
• See how it may come across: warm, tense, escalating, flat, soft or unsure, with the reasons.
• Get one concrete tip for next time.

PRACTICE AND PREP
• Short drills with clear goals: slow down, stay steady under pressure, put feeling into your voice.
• Prep a hard conversation: build a calm opening line, catch blame phrases like "you always," and rehearse it out loud.
• Track your progress and keep a daily practice streak.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your audio is analyzed on your phone and is never uploaded.
• No account, no ads, no trackers. We never sell data.
• Each partner checks only their own voice.

HONEST ABOUT LIMITS
Talya hears how you speak, not what you mean. It can be wrong, it can't detect sarcasm, and it never decides who is right. It is a self-awareness tool, not therapy or medical advice.

## Google Play: Data safety answers
- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No.**
- Reason: audio is processed only on the device and never sent off it. Google does not count data that is processed only on the device as "collected."
- Is data encrypted in transit? Not applicable (nothing is transmitted).
- Can users request deletion? Users can delete all local data in the app (Our space → Delete everything).

## Notes for the Apple reviewer
Talya analyzes the user's own voice on the device to describe how their tone may come across (pace, pitch, loudness, pauses). To test: open the app, follow "Get started" (tap Record step 1, read the line, tap Stop; repeat for step 2), then view the result. No account is needed. Microphone audio is never uploaded. Practice drills and conversation prep also use the microphone. Progress is stored locally.
